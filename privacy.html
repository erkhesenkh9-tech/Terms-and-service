Overview

Vezo helps people discover events in the San Francisco Bay Area. This policy explains what we collect and your choices.

What we collect

Account: display name, username, birth year, optional profile photo, and email if you sign up with email or Google. Usage: events, RSVPs, posts, memories, friends, and reports. We do not collect continuous location; optional “locate me” stays on your device.

What we do NOT collect

Legal name, home address, school, phone number, gender, contacts, or background location tracking.

How we use data

To operate the app, improve safety, and comply with law. Some events are imported from Ticketmaster with source attribution.

Sharing

Public profile fields are visible to members. We use Supabase for hosting and do not sell personal information.

Deletion

Delete your account anytime in Settings with email verification.
