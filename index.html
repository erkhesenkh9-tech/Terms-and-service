The map

See your city differently.

Events live where they happen. Pan across the Bay and the map fills in around you — each pin an event you can open, read, and say you're going to.

How it works

From a pin to a night out.

  1. Discover

    Open the map and see what's on near you, right now or later this week.

  2. Join

    Tap that you're going. It takes one tap and no account — make one when you want friends, posts or events of your own.

  3. Experience

    Show up. Every event has a chat room for the people going, so you're not arriving cold.

  4. Remember

    Post a photo or a clip from the night, and keep the events you went to saved to your profile.

Create

Have something happening? Put it on the map.

Name it, drop a pin, pick a time. Make it public for the whole Bay Area, or invite-only for the people you choose. Every event comes with a guest list, co-hosts and a chat room for everyone going.

Events are posted by people in the community. Vezo doesn't verify or endorse them — check the details with the organiser before you go.

The event page

Everything about the night, in one place.

When, where, who's hosting and how many people are expected. Say you're going in a tap, or save it for later. The event chat opens as the day gets close, so you're not turning up cold.

Community

The event is the point. The people come with it.

Add friends, start a group chat, and plan the weekend in one thread. Post from an event while it's happening, and send anything worth seeing straight to a friend.

Memories

Events leave the map. The night doesn't have to.

When an event is over it comes off the map — but the photos and clips from it stay with you, saved privately to the events you went to.

Trust & safety

Real events, real people.

Vezo sends people to places in the real world, so what gets onto the map matters. Here is what we actually do about it.

  • Photos are reviewed

    Every uploaded photo is checked before it can appear anywhere in the app.

  • Report and block

    Events, posts and comments can be reported in two taps, and anyone can be blocked from contacting you.

  • Rules that are enforced

    Fake events, spam, scams, harassment and unsafe content aren't allowed. Accounts that break the rules are suspended.

  • Events can't vanish

    An event with people attending can't be quietly deleted out from under them.

Some listings are imported from third parties, including Ticketmaster, SeatGeek and Funcheap SF. Vezo can't guarantee the safety, legitimacy or accuracy of every event — check the details with the organiser, use your judgement, and report anything that looks wrong.

Privacy

Location when you need it. Not when you don't.

Vezo doesn't collect continuous location, and there's no background tracking. The optional "locate me" on the map stays on your device.

  • Sharing is opt-in

    Sharing your location at an event is off until you turn it on, limited to the audience you pick, and expires within two minutes unless you refresh it. It stops on its own when the event ends.

  • Less about you

    No legal name, home address, phone number, gender or contacts. We don't sell personal information.

  • Leave whenever

    Delete your account any time from Settings, with email verification.

  • Read the detail

    The privacy policy is short, and it is the authority on all of this.

FAQ

Questions.

What is Vezo?

A map-first app for finding events near you. Instead of scrolling a directory, you see what's happening around you on a map, open anything that looks good, and say you're going.

Where can I use it?

Vezo covers San Francisco, Oakland, Berkeley and the rest of the Bay Area. It's currently a Bay Area beta, so features may change.

Do I need an account?

Not to look around, and not to say you're going — that takes one tap and no sign-up. You'll need an account for friends, posting, and creating events of your own.

Can I create an event?

Yes. Name it, drop a pin and pick a time. You choose whether it's public for the whole Bay Area or invite-only, and it comes with a guest list, co-hosts and a chat room.

Does Vezo track my location?

No. Vezo doesn't collect continuous location and doesn't track you in the background. The optional "locate me" on the map stays on your device.

Sharing your location at an event is a separate, opt-in choice: it's off until you turn it on, limited to the audience you pick, and expires within two minutes unless you refresh it.

Where do the listings come from?

Two places: events created by people in the community, and listings imported from third-party sources including Ticketmaster, SeatGeek and Funcheap SF. Imported events carry their source. Verify details with the organiser before you go.

How old do I need to be?

You must be at least 13 to use Vezo. See the Terms of Service.

What is Vezo Pro?

An optional subscription — $2.99 per month or $19.99 per year, starting with a 7-day free trial. It adds event themes, smart alerts for the categories you follow, a boosted map pin, organiser analytics, a higher event-creation limit, and profile themes. Everything else in Vezo is free.

Where are the community rules?

In the Community Guidelines. They cover what's allowed on the map, how reporting works, and what happens when someone breaks the rules.
